Privacy Policy

Duotext. Updated 8 October 2026.

Your books, translations and saved words stay on your device. Anonymous purchase, usage and Apple Ads attribution data are described below.

What the app stores

  • books and texts you import: EPUB and TXT files, pasted text, articles saved by link;
  • translations the app makes for you, and your reading position;
  • words you save, together with the sentences they came from;
  • reading settings: theme, font, type size, translation mode.

All of this is stored locally on the device, in the app's protected container. There is no account and no server of ours behind the app.

Translation happens on the device

Duotext translates with Apple's built-in translation system that runs on the device itself. The text of your books is not sent to us or to any third-party service. Language packs are downloaded to your device by iOS from Apple.

Importing by link

When you save an article by link, the app downloads that page directly from the website you specified — the same way a browser would. No intermediary server of ours is involved.

Custom covers

If you set a book cover from your photos, the system photo picker hands the app only the image you chose. The app has no access to the rest of your photo library. The image is stored locally with the book.

What we do not do

  • we do not collect your name, email, phone number or anything else that identifies you;
  • the app does not display third-party advertising;
  • we never sell your data, and we never share the content of your books, your translations or your saved words with anyone — anonymous purchase events, usage statistics and Apple Ads attribution data are described below;
  • we do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your card details. The app only sees whether Pro is active. Managing and cancelling a subscription happens in your Apple ID settings.

To understand purchase statistics (how many trials start, how many convert) the app reports purchase events to RevenueCat, a purchases-analytics service, under a random identifier generated on your device. What is sent: which product was bought, when, in which country and currency, and whether a trial or a renewal took place — this is the purchase history category in Apple's privacy labels. What is never sent: your name, your email, your device identifiers, and anything from your books, translations or saved words. The identifier is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across apps or websites. RevenueCat's own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics

To learn whether the introduction and the Pro offer are clear, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to RevenueCat under the same random identifier as purchase events. What is sent: how far you went through the introduction, whether you revealed the first translation, which book language and translation language you chose there, how many seconds the steps took, what happened on the Pro offer (closed, picked a plan, started a trial, bought, restored), and whether the app asked you for an App Store rating. This is the "product interaction" category in Apple's privacy labels. It is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

What is never sent: your name, your email, your device identifiers, the text of your books, your translations, the words you save and anything you type. What you read and which words you save never leaves the device.

Apple Ads attribution

From version 1.5.1, to understand whether Apple Ads brings paying customers, the app sends a Standard AdServices attribution token to RevenueCat. RevenueCat requests attribution data from Apple and associates the available campaign, ad group, keyword and country or region with the same random app identifier used for purchase analytics. This is the advertising data category in Apple’s privacy labels, used for analytics. No IDFA or IDFV is collected by the app. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not send your books, translations or saved words. This feature uses Standard attribution and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
