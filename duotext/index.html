Duotext — Support

A reader with subtitles for books: the original line on top, its translation right underneath. Translation happens on your iPhone — offline and private.

Frequently asked

Why is there no translation under the text?

Open the book menu (•••) and check «Translation». If it is set to «On tap», tap the small circle at the end of a line. Also make sure the translation language differs from the language of the book — the app will tell you if they match.

Which files can I import?

EPUB and TXT files, pasted text, and articles from the web by link.

Does translation need the internet?

No. Translation runs on the device. The first time a language pair is used, iOS downloads the language pack — after that everything works offline.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings inside the app and tap «Restore purchases» while signed in with the same Apple ID.
