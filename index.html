Frequent questions

Where are my tasks stored?

Only on your device. We do not collect and do not send anywhere anything you create in the app — details in the privacy policy.

Reminders don't arrive

Check Settings → Notifications on your phone: the permission may not have been granted at the first prompt. Banners and sound should be on there too.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You can cancel at any time; the period you already paid for runs to its end.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap “Restore purchases” on the subscription screen.

How do I delete all data?

You can clear the contents inside the app, in its settings. Deleting the app erases all of its data from the device.
