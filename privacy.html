Privacy Policy

Roomdo. Updated 29 September 2026.

Roomdo is a visual planner. Core tasks, notes, photos and rooms are stored on your device and, if enabled, in your private iCloud database. Roomdo 2.0 adds optional AI object creation. That feature sends the selected task text to our service and an AI provider only after you agree in the app.

Your tasks and iCloud

Task names, dates, reminders, notes, checklists, personal photos, room settings and generated objects are stored in the app’s protected container. If iCloud is enabled, this content can sync through the same Apple account’s private iCloud database. We do not receive a copy of your entire planner or have access to your private iCloud database. Roomdo 2.0 does not have a separate Roomdo login.

Optional AI objects

When you choose to create an object, we show a consent notice before the first request. The text of that task is sent over HTTPS to our service hosted by Yandex Cloud in Kazakhstan. A new image is generated by OpenAI when a suitable unused object is not available in our library. Notes, personal photos, checklists and other tasks are not included in the generation request.

Please avoid including personal, confidential or sensitive details in a task you send for AI generation. A generic description is sufficient. OpenAI processes the request under its privacy policy and API data controls.

Our service temporarily stores the task text while a request is being processed. After success or failure, the raw text is cleared from the job record. A keyed hash of the normalized text remains so that matching tasks can receive existing artwork. Hashes are not published and are not shown as a list of your tasks.

Generated object images are kept in a shared library and may be supplied to other users. The library does not share your original task title, notes, photos or identity. We store delivery history linked to an app-specific identifier to avoid sending you the same variant again and to charge each request only once. Deleting a task from your device does not automatically delete the reusable object from the shared library.

Purchases and tokens

Apple processes payments; we never receive payment card details. RevenueCat supports purchase measurement and entitlement management. It receives an app-generated customer identifier, purchase information and the usage counters below.

For AI tokens, our server verifies Apple-signed app and purchase transactions. It stores app-specific identifiers, transaction identifiers, product and subscription status, token grants, balance and spending history. We use these records to deliver purchases, restore balances, handle refunds and prevent duplicate charges or abuse. This is not your Apple account password or email address.

Purchase and balance records are retained as needed to fulfil purchases and subscriptions, handle refunds and satisfy applicable recordkeeping obligations. Generated objects and their matching hashes remain while they support the library. Limited backup copies may remain after records are removed from the active service.

Usage measurement

Roomdo records onboarding, paywall, purchase and feature-use counters with the RevenueCat customer identifier. Counters do not contain task names, notes, photos or room names. We also measure generation status, provider usage and costs to operate the service. We do not use advertising networks, sell personal data or track people across unrelated apps and websites.

Notifications and permissions

Task reminders are local notifications. Permission is requested when needed and can be withdrawn in system settings. Personal photos are selected using Apple’s system picker. Saving an image to Photos requests add-only access when that action is chosen.

Your choices

You can use the core planner without AI generation and decline the generation consent notice. You can delete local content in the app and control iCloud in system settings. Subscription cancellation is managed through Apple. For questions or requests concerning server-held generation or purchase data, contact saverinkz@yandex.ru. We may need information sufficient to verify the relevant app purchase or generation record before acting. Some purchase records may need to be retained for refund handling or legal obligations.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
